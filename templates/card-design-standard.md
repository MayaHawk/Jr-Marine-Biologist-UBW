# Card Design Standard

**Set by Maya:** 2026-09-19 · **Owner:** Product & Curriculum Manager
**Applies to:** every activity card, in all 24 topics
**Status:** ✅ CONFIRMED by Maya — this is the standard every set must match

> **Why this matters beyond looks.** When every card marks its materials the same way, the materials list stops guessing. The flags sheet in Maya's own master list records scissors marked "not included" on some cards and not others, and cotton balls appearing as both. **This standard is what ends that.**

---

## FRONT OF CARD

> 🔴 **The photo must be a real marine photograph.** This is not a style preference — it is the brand. `company/brand-voice-guide.md` says the brand must read as *"created by real marine educators"* and must never look like a generic craft box. An illustrated or stock-cartoon front undoes that in the half-second before anyone reads a word. A real photograph of a real animal is the fastest possible signal that a marine scientist made this.
>
> **Sourcing is an open question:** Maya's own photography, licensed stock, or public-domain science imagery (NOAA and similar)? Every image needs usage rights confirmed before print. Logged as N-16.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Photo** | 🔴 **A REAL MARINE PHOTOGRAPH.** Confirmed 2026-09-19 — not an illustration, not clip art, not AI-generated imagery. Full-bleed. |
| ❌ **No title on the photo** | The activity title does **not** appear on the front |
| **Only two pieces of text** | The **topic name** and the **card number** |
| Card number format | `Card 3 of 5` *(see confirmation below)* |

**That is everything on the front.** Photo, topic, card number. Nothing else.

---

## BACK OF CARD

### 1. Activity title — **in bold**

### 2. Steps
The activity, written as steps.

### 3. Materials — in two blocks, in this order

**Block 1 — what's in the kit** *(normal text)*
> Listed plainly. These are the items packed in the box.

**Block 2 — NOT INCLUDED** *(at the bottom, italicised)*
> *Everything the parent supplies from home goes here.* Scissors, a bowl of water, a timer, spoons, a tub, a couch — **all of it goes at the bottom, in italics, under "not included".**

---

## The rule, stated once

> **If it does not go in the box, it goes at the bottom in italics under NOT INCLUDED. Every card. No exceptions.**

This is what lets the materials list be built automatically and trusted. A card that lists scissors in the main block creates a phantom item that gets costed, ordered and packed — or worse, a parent who thinks scissors are coming and finds none.

---

## Checklist — run this on every card before it goes to print

**Front**
- [ ] Photo present, full-bleed
- [ ] **No activity title on the photo**
- [ ] Topic name present
- [ ] Card number present, in the `X of 5` format
- [ ] Nothing else on the front

**Back**
- [ ] Activity title present and **bold**
- [ ] Steps written out
- [ ] Kit materials listed in the main block, normal text
- [ ] **NOT INCLUDED block at the bottom, italicised**
- [ ] Every household item is in the NOT INCLUDED block — scissors, bowls, water, timers, spoons, containers
- [ ] Nothing appears in both blocks
- [ ] Quantities given wherever a count matters

**Cross-check**
- [ ] The card's materials match the topic's row in the master materials list
- [ ] Any item without a stated quantity is flagged, not assumed
