## What this tool actually does — plain language

The GoDaddy SEO tool does **not** change your website. You give it a list of search phrases, and it:
1. tells you roughly how many people search each phrase and how hard it is to rank for,
2. tracks where your site currently sits in Google results for each one,
3. suggests page changes you can choose to apply.

So the keyword list is a **measuring instrument**, not a marketing action. Nothing gets published by entering it.
