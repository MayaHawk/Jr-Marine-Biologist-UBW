# Goals & Metrics

**Last updated:** 2026-08-18
**Rule:** every recommendation any agent makes must connect to one of the metrics on this page. If a recommendation cannot name the metric it moves, it is not ready.

---

## The starting business goal (CONFIRMED)

> Build a reliable system for attracting qualified customers and increasing paid Junior Marine Biologist kit subscriptions, while maintaining healthy profit margins and delivering an excellent educational experience.

Read carefully, that goal has **four** parts, and all four must hold at once:

| Part | Means |
|---|---|
| **Reliable system** | Repeatable and documented, not one-off bursts of effort |
| **Qualified customers** | People who will actually stay subscribed — not just traffic |
| **Increasing paid subscriptions** | Paid, recurring. The headline number. |
| **Healthy margins + excellent education** | Growth that loses money or lowers quality does not count as progress |

**Active paid subscribers.**

## The health guardrails

A plan that hits the subscriber number but breaks one of these is **not** a good plan, and the Chief of Staff must say so:

1. **Gross margin must stay healthy.** Target to be set by Maya once real COGS is known.
2. **LTV must comfortably exceed CAC.** *(A commonly cited benchmark is 3:1 — labeled ASSUMPTION until validated for this business.)*
3. **Churn must not rise** as subscriber count grows.
4. **Educational quality must not fall** to cut cost. Product & Curriculum Manager can veto on this basis and escalate to Maya.
5. **Fulfillment must keep up.** Operations must confirm feasibility before any growth campaign is approved.
6. **Cash must not run out.** Physical products consume cash before they produce it.

## How to write a goal so it counts

> **Bad:** "Grow on Instagram."
> **Good:** "Add 200 email subscribers in 30 days from Instagram, measured in the email platform, to feed the shark-kit launch in month two."

Every goal needs: a **number**, a **deadline**, a **way to measure it**, and a **reason it matters**.
