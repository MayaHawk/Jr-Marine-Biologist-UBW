# Which Agents for Which Job

**Purpose:** stop every agent from working on every task. The Chief of Staff uses this to assign the **minimum** set of agents a job actually needs.

**Why it matters:** eight agents on a two-agent job produces a long document, contradictory advice, and a buried answer. Fewer agents, better briefed, gives Maya a faster and clearer decision.

**Overlap rules:**
- **Pricing** — Finance owns the number. Sales proposes and packages it. **Maya approves it.** Nobody else states a price.
- **Product facts in copy** — Product owns the truth. Social and Sales must use their file, never their own memory.
- **Ship dates** — Operations owns the date. Sales and Social may only repeat an Operations-confirmed date.
- **Retention** — Customer Experience owns the experience; Sales owns the offer inside it.

## Sizing the team for a job

| Job type | Agents | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Single question** | 1 | "What's our break-even?" → Finance |
| **Two-step** | 2 | "Research shark demand and tell me if the kit works" → Research → Product |
| **Campaign** | 3–4 | Product → Finance → Sales → Social |
| **Launch** | 5–6 | Product, Finance, Ops, Sales, Social, CX |
| **Major strategic decision** | all 8 | "Should we add an annual plan?" |

**Default to fewer.** Add an agent when a specific question genuinely belongs to them — not "just in case."

---

## Test before assigning

The Chief of Staff asks, for each candidate agent:

1. **Does this job contain a question this agent uniquely owns?** If no — don't assign.
2. **Would their absence make the recommendation wrong or incomplete?** If no — don't assign.
3. **Do they have the inputs they need to answer?** If no — get the input first, or the output is guesswork.

Three noes means the agent stays out.

## Sequencing

Some agents must come **after** others or they produce fiction:

| Agent | Must come after | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | Product | No supply list means no COGS |
| Sales | Finance | An offer without a margin is a guess |
| Social | Sales + Product | Campaigns need a real offer and real product facts |
| Operations | Product | Nothing to source or pack without a supply list |
| Customer Experience | Sales + Ops | Onboarding depends on the offer and the ship timeline |

**Running these out of order is the single most common way this team produces confident nonsense.**

Agents that can run **in parallel**: Market Researcher works independently at the start; Finance and Operations can run together once the supply list exists; Sales and Social can draft together once price and product are confirmed.
