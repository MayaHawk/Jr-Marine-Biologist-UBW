## 3. RUNNING LATE ⏳
*Send BEFORE the customer has to ask. A proactive delay email almost never causes a cancellation. Silence does.*

> **Subject:** A quick, honest update on your kit
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> I want to get ahead of this rather than leave you wondering: your [KIT NAME] kit is running a few days behind. [ONE HONEST SENTENCE ON WHY — e.g. "a supplier shipment arrived late."]
>
> **New ship date:** [DATE] — and you'll get a tracking number the moment it goes out.
>
> I'm sorry for the wait. [MAYA: your call — do you want to add anything here? A bonus item, a small credit, or nothing at all? Delete this line before sending.]
>
> Thank you for your patience,
> Maya
