**Prepared:** 2026-09-19 · **By:** Finance Manager
> ⚠️ **Internal planning only. Not tax, accounting, or legal advice. For review by a licensed professional.**

| Line | Amount | Confidence |

**Still to come out of that $37.92:** activity materials for all five STEAM projects · postage (free to the customer, so absorbed) · certificate printing · instruction guide · packing materials · label · and Maya's own labour, which this sheet does not count.

### The honest read
**$45.99 leaves real room.** At the August research price, a kit is not obviously underwater — which was a genuine worry when the price was unknown. **But no margin figure can be stated until activity materials and postage land**, and those are the two biggest unknowns left.

**The fastest way to close this:** the Click-N-Ship export gives real postage, and one topic's activity costs gives the rest. Two jobs, and the margin stops being a guess.

## On the "$10K in a week" goal

**ESTIMATE — illustrative, because margin is still unknown:**

| If gross margin is | Sales needed to net $10,000 | Kits at $45.99 |
|---|---|---|
| 50% | $20,000 | ~435 |
| 40% | $25,000 | ~544 |
| 30% | $33,333 | ~725 |

For scale: the site had **107 users in 28 days** and GA4 recorded **zero purchases**. Moving from that to several hundred kits in a week is not a marketing-tuning problem — it is a different order of magnitude.

**This is not a reason to abandon the target.** It is a reason to be precise about what "10K" means — revenue or profit, and over what period — and to notice that **the 781-contact warm list is by far the most likely source of it.** Those are people who already know and trust Maya.

**NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT:** does "$10K" mean revenue or profit in your pocket?
