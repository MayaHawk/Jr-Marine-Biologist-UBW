## 🔴 FINDING 1 — You may be overpaying on every single box

**CONFIRMED (Maya, 2026-08-18):** boxes ship via **USPS Click-N-Ship**.
**CONFIRMED:** you offer **free US shipping**, so every shipping dollar comes straight out of your margin.

Click-N-Ship's most-used service is **Priority Mail**. But USPS also offers **Ground Advantage**, and research is consistent that it is materially cheaper for light packages:

| Service | 1 lb, Zone 1 (retail) | 2 lb, Zone 1 (retail) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ground Advantage** | ~$8.85 | ~$10.00 |
| **Priority Mail** | higher — commercial rates run ~$9.04 vs ~$7.61 for Ground Advantage on the same 1 lb parcel | higher |

> *"Ground Advantage is cheaper than Priority Mail for the vast majority of packages — often by $2 to $12 at July 2026 prices."* — ship.com, searched 2026-08-18
> *"Ground Advantage saves 25–40% compared to Priority Mail on the same package, with savings growing with distance."* — atoship.com, searched 2026-08-18

**Both include tracking and $100 insurance.** The trade is speed: Ground Advantage 2–5 business days, Priority Mail 1–3.

### What this could mean — ESTIMATE, illustrative only
If you ship a 2 lb kit and switch from Priority Mail to Ground Advantage at a saving of **$2 per box** *(the low end of the researched range)*:

| Kits per month | Monthly saving | Annual saving |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | $50 | $600 |
| 50 | $100 | $1,200 |
| 100 | $200 | $2,400 |

**These are illustrations built on an unverified $2 figure, not a promise.** Your Click-N-Ship history shows what you actually paid — the real number is one export away.

### ✅ Do this first — it costs nothing and takes 10 minutes
1. Sign in at **usps.com** → **Click-N-Ship** → **Shipping History**
2. Export or screenshot the last 20–30 labels
3. Note the **service used**, the **price paid**, and the **weight** on each

That gives you your **true cost per box** and your **true box weight** — two Priority 0 unknowns, answered from data you already own. Send them here and Finance will calculate your real shipping cost per kit the same day.

**Also worth checking:** Click-N-Ship pricing versus a discounted-postage service. Those routinely offer below-retail commercial rates. **This has not been researched yet** and is listed as follow-up work — no service is being recommended.

### 1b. Mailer box — earlier RESEARCHED RANGE, kept for reference

| Option | Range per unit | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Plain kraft mailer, at volume | **under ~$0.60** | Cheapest. No branding. |
| Custom printed, 100–500 units | **~$1.30–$3.80** | Small-run pricing |
| Custom printed, general | **~$1.50–$4.50** | Varies by size and finish |
| Corrugated, 100–500/month | **~$0.75–$2.50** | |

**Setup costs to know about:** a die-cut tool runs roughly **$150–$450**, print setup roughly **$80–$1,500**. Spread over 100 boxes that adds about **$3 per box**; over 5,000 boxes, about **6 cents**. Digital printing has no setup cost, which is why it wins on small runs.

**Minimum orders:** most suppliers want 500–1,000 for stock sizes with custom print; fully custom sizes often start at 1,000–2,500. Some accept 100.

*Sources: ecopackables.com, customboxmakers.com, refinepackaging.com, brandtbox.com, packaura.com — searched 2026-08-18*

> **Judgment call for Maya:** at your current volume, a **plain mailer with a printed sticker or label** likely beats a custom-printed box on cost, while still looking deliberate. Custom printing earns its keep at volume. This is a recommendation to consider, not a decision.
