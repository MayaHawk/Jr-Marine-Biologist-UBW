## 🔴 Read this first — what this file is, and what it is not

**This file flags. It does not clear.**

- ❌ **No claim is made, in either direction, that any Junior Marine Biologist kit or component meets or fails CPSIA, ASTM F963, CE marking, any "non-toxic" certification, or any age rating.** **Maya has confirmed no testing of any kind has been done.**
- **Absence of testing is not a finding that something is unsafe.** It is an absence of information, and it must be described that way everywhere — in this file, in marketing, and to any buyer who asks.
- **An age band printed on a card (4–6, 7–10, 10+) is our own audience guidance. It is not a certified age grade** and must never be presented as one.
- ✅ What this team **may** and **does** write are **practical notes**: *"Contains small parts — adult supervision recommended for children under 8."* Practical guidance is not a rating.

> **When anyone asks whether a kit is compliant, the answer is: that needs verification with a children's product safety professional. Here is what I have flagged.**

**Two confirmed facts that sit behind everything below** (`company/known-facts.md`): **there is no product liability insurance**, and **supplies have been ordered from Temu**, where safety documentation and supplier traceability are rarely available *(risk R-01, open question U-20)*.
