# product-development/ — Kits, Lessons & Curriculum

**Owner:** `product-curriculum-manager`

## What belongs here
Per-topic kit specifications · the topic roadmap · lesson plans · flashcard content · worksheets and activities · supply lists · the science verification log · safety notes · digital product specs.

## Structure
```
product-development/
├── topics/       ← one file per marine science topic (the ~24)
├── lessons/
├── flashcards/
├── activities/
├── supply-lists/ ← feeds finance (COGS) and operations (sourcing)
├── digital/
├── science-verification-log.md   ← claims Maya must verify
├── safety-notes.md
└── topic-roadmap.md
```

> The **master product list** lives in `company/product-catalog.md`, not here. This folder holds the detail; that file holds the truth.

## The rules for this folder
1. **Never invent a marine science fact.** Flag it `[VERIFY]` and log it. Maya is the marine scientist — she verifies.
2. **Never claim an educational or safety standard** unless Maya has confirmed it.
3. **Always flag safety hazards** — small parts, sharp edges, liquids, allergens.
