## Age 7–9 — the five shots already specified

| Card | Discipline | Photo needed |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Science | **Close-up of a shark's snout showing its pores** *(the Ampullae of Lorenzini — the card's own subject)* |
| **2** | Science | **Shark gill slits, up close** |
| **3** | Engineering | **Diver observing a shark from a shark cage** |
| **4** | Math | **Whale shark swimming beside a diver for scale** |
| **5** | Art | **Megalodon tooth beside a modern great white tooth** |

**Each shot teaches the card's point rather than decorating it** — pores for electroreception, a diver for scale, two teeth for comparison. That is a genuinely good brief, and worth keeping when the photos are sourced.
