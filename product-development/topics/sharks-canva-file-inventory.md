## B. 🔴 THE BIG GAP — an Age 4–6 tier exists and is NOT in the ALL set

| Design | id | pp |
|---|---|---|

| Making Sense Of Sharks **Age 4-6** *(duplicate)* | `DAHTy3KXpiU` | 15 |
| Making Sense Of Sharks **Age 4-6 (Digital)** | `DAHT3oLlfyM` | 14 |

**The ALL set carries only two tiers — 7–9 and 10+.** But a complete Age 4–6 Sharks tier exists as its own design, and Maya's own August materials sheet had three columns: `AGE 4-6 / 7-10 / 10+`.

**This matters commercially.** The audience is stated as ages 4–12. If the ALL set is the single source for production, **the youngest band of the audience has no cards in it** — for the best-selling topic.

> **NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT:** is Age 4–6 deliberately excluded from the kit, sold separately, or simply not yet merged? This decides SKU count, print run and what "ages 4–12" can honestly claim.
