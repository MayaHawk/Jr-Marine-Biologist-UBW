## What the source revealed

### 1. Every kit follows a STEAM structure — CONFIRMED
Each of the 5 activities maps to one letter:

| Letter | Stands for | Example (Sharks) |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | Science | How Do Sharks Breathe? |
| **T** | Technology | Sharks' Magnetic Sense |
| **E** | Engineering | Build Your Own Shark Cage |
| **A** | Art | Make Your Own Shark Tooth Necklace |
| **M** | Math | Measuring Sharks |

**This is a genuine brand asset that is currently invisible.** "Five activities" says much less than "one Science, one Technology, one Engineering, one Art, and one Math activity in every kit" — which is exactly the language homeschool parents, co-ops and teachers search for and buy on. Flagged to Sales and Social.

### 2. Kits are age-tiered — three bands
`AGE 4–6` · `AGE 7–10` · `AGE 10+`

For Sharks, all three columns are filled and the materials are **largely identical**, differing in small ways (a wire wrap only at 10+, hemp string instead of yarn at 7–10). For Sea Turtles and Coral Reefs, **only the 10+ column is populated** in the source.

**NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT:** are three genuinely different kits packed and shipped, or is it one kit with age-adapted instructions? The answer changes inventory, packing time and SKU count by roughly 3×.

### 3. Packing instruction that appears on every kit
> **"pack in box not plastic bag"**

Now carried into the packing SOP.
