### 🟢 The finding that matters most for cost
**9–12 items per kit are NOT purchased or packed** — the parent already has them: bowls of water, scissors, a timer, spoons, a couch, a phone camera, table salt, tall cups.

**That is a real and previously uncounted reduction in cost per kit.** Every margin estimate the team has made assumed all activity materials were bought. A meaningful share are not.

**It is also a design strength worth selling:** a kit that uses a bowl of water from the kitchen feels like real science, not a sealed plastic experience.

## E. 🟢 A safety decision already made — recorded here

**2026-09-16:** the 10+ "Diving Body" card was rewritten to remove the face-in-the-bowl step, replaced with balloon + sponge + cold washcloth. The 7–9 Sperm Whale card was changed to the same method the same day.

> **Standing rule, from the source sheet: no face-in-water, no breath-holding.**

This is exactly the kind of judgement the Product & Curriculum Manager exists to protect. **Recorded in `company/known-facts.md` so it is never quietly reversed.**

## F. How this sheet is built — worth copying for the other 19 topics

1. **A "by card" sheet per topic**, listing every item per activity per age tier
2. **A master sheet** that rolls those up, taking **the most any single card needs** — not the sum — because one ruler is reused across cards
3. **A household-items sheet** for the parent note
4. **A flags sheet** recording every assumption and inconsistency found

**Quantities flow from the by-card sheets, so editing one number updates the master.** That is a well-built document.
