> ### ⚠️ IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER TEAM FINDING
> The team previously treated the shark tooth necklace as a **finished fossil-tooth necklace** and raised sharp-edge and small-part safety concerns on that basis.
>
> **The source shows the Art activity is MAKE-YOUR-OWN, from air dry clay, yarn and toothpicks.** A clay tooth the child moulds themselves carries a very different risk profile from a real fossil tooth — the sharp-edge concern largely falls away.
>
> **BUT** the pack-in list *also* separately lists `* shark tooth necklace *  | 1` as an included item. So the kit may contain **both** a make-your-own activity **and** a finished necklace.
>
> **NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT — this is the single most important open question on this kit:** is the included `shark tooth necklace` a real fossil tooth, a replica, or the clay one the child makes? The answer changes the cost, the sourcing, and the safety picture.
