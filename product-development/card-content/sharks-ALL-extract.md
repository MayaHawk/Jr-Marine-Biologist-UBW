# 🔴 FINDINGS — flagged for Maya

## 1. The shark tooth necklace question is ANSWERED
Open question **U-28** has been unresolved since August: is the packed necklace a real fossil tooth, a replica, or the clay one the child makes?

**Both, in different tiers:**
- **Younger tier:** Art card 5 — the child **makes** a tooth from air-dry clay, yarn and a toothpick. No real tooth.
- **Age 10+:** Science card 1 — materials list **"Shark tooth (fossil or replica)"**. A physical tooth is supplied.

**Consequences:**
- The earlier safety concern about sharp fossil edges **applies only to the 10+ tier.**
- The earlier cost concern **also applies only to 10+.** The younger tier's necklace costs a piece of clay.
- **NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT:** fossil or replica? They differ in cost, sourcing and safety.

## 2. 🔴 The younger tier is NOT STEAM — it has two Science cards and no Technology
| Tier | S | T | E | A | M |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Younger** | Magnetic Sense | ❌ **missing** | Shark Cage | Tooth Necklace | Measuring Sharks |
| | *(How Do Sharks Breathe is also Science)* | | | | |
| **Age 10+** | Cartilage | Acoustic Arrays | Fins and Lift | Countershading | Population Curve |

**The 10+ tier is properly STEAM. The younger tier is not.**

This matters commercially: the STEAM structure is one of the strongest selling points for school and homeschool buyers, and the catalog states every kit has one of each. **On the younger Sharks tier that is not currently true.** Either card 1 or card 2 would need reframing as Technology, or one replaced.

## 3. 🔴 The 10+ cards have no photos placed
Every 10+ card carries placeholder text: *"Drop photo: shark skeleton or jaw showing rows of teeth"*, *"Drop background photo"*. **The 10+ tier cannot print.** Maya's rule is a real marine photograph on the front — these have none.

## 4. Materials formatting does not yet meet the card standard
Cards currently write *"scissors (not included)"* **inline**, e.g. `Coffee filter · rubber band · paint · salt · googly eyes · cup and clear jar (not included)`.

The standard (D-015) requires not-included items **at the bottom, in italics, in their own block.** **Every Sharks card needs reformatting.**

## 5. ⚠️ Card numbering says "No. 1 of 5", not "Card 1 of 5"
Maya confirmed the wording as **`Card [N] of 5`**. The existing cards read **`Sharks · Science · No. 1 of 5`**.

**NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT:** change existing cards to "Card", or was "No." intended? The current format also carries the **discipline name** (Science, Math, Art…), which the standard does not mention — keep it? It is a genuine asset for school buyers.

## 6. Editor artifacts in the file
The words **"Replace"** and **"Edit"** appear in the 10+ pages — Canva editor artifacts. Must not reach print.
