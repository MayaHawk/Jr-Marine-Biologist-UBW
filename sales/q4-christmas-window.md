**Raised by Maya:** 2026-09-19 · **Owner:** Chief of Staff, with Sales and Operations

## B. 🔴 The deadline is not Christmas. It is December 17.

**Stock must be built before orders arrive, not after.** A gift that ships on December 20 is a refund request, not a sale — and this business does not give refunds (D-012), which makes a missed Christmas delivery far worse than an ordinary late order.

**Build time for Christmas inventory** *(assembly time still unmeasured — this is why it matters)*:

| Kits | at 10 min | at 20 min | at 30 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 17 hrs | 33 hrs | 50 hrs |
| 200 | 33 hrs | 67 hrs | **100 hrs** |
| 300 | 50 hrs | **100 hrs** | **150 hrs — nearly 4 weeks** |
| 500 | 83 hrs | **167 hrs** | **250 hrs — over 6 weeks** |

**Whether Christmas is a 100-kit or a 500-kit opportunity depends entirely on a number nobody has measured.**

## D. 🎁 Digital is the Q4 weapon, and it is currently broken

| | Physical kit | Digital pack / Library |
|---|---|---|
| Last order date | ~Dec 17 | **Dec 24, 11:59pm** |
| Cost to fulfil | box, postage, packing, assembly | **≈ zero** |
| Assembly time | unmeasured | none |
| Margin | unknown | **≈100%** |

**Last-minute gift buyers are the least price-sensitive customers of the year.** Somebody on December 23 with a child to buy for is not comparing prices — they need something good, now. A **$99 Complete Digital Library**, delivered instantly, with a printable gift certificate, is close to a perfect product for that moment.

> 🔴 **17 of the 24 digital packs currently carry the wrong PDF.** They are hidden, so nothing is being sold wrongly — but it means the highest-margin, deadline-free, zero-fulfilment product line is **sitting idle heading into the one season it was made for.** This is now the single largest missed-revenue risk on the board.

## E. What Christmas changes about the strategy

**It does not replace the bulk plan — it runs alongside it.**

| | Bulk channels | Christmas |
|---|---|---|
| Who | camps, co-ops, museums, schools | parents, grandparents, gift buyers |
| Timing | camps buy Jan–Apr; lands mid-2027 | **now through Dec 24** |
| Volume | 50–300 per buyer | 1–3 per buyer, but many buyers |
| Effort | relationship by relationship | one campaign, many recipients |

**Gift buyers are already a confirmed audience segment.** The 781-contact warm list is made of parents who already trust Maya. **Q4 is the natural moment to reach them** — and it needs no new relationships, no purchase orders, and no insurance certificate.

### Two things Christmas does that bulk cannot
1. **It converts the warm list now**, rather than waiting for a camp director's budget cycle.
2. **Local delivery becomes a genuine gift feature.** "Hand-delivered before Christmas by a marine biologist" is not something a national kit company can offer at any price.

---

## F. ⚠️ The honest risk

**A one-person operation taking Christmas orders it cannot build is the worst outcome available.** Late gifts, no refunds, and the reviews that follow would cost more than the revenue gained.

This business has already had a customer wait 50 days. **Christmas is unforgiving in a way ordinary orders are not** — the deadline is absolute.

**Therefore:** decide a realistic number of kits to sell, build them **before** promoting, and **cap the offer** when stock runs out. A sold-out Christmas is a good problem. An oversold one is not.
