The goal D-010 has a tension baked into it: *as quickly as possible* pulls toward discounts, *for as much as possible* pulls against them. **Local delivery is the only move in this plan that improves both sides at once.**

## 2. The three rings

### 🟢 RING 1 — drivable same day · **highest priority**
**Delray Beach · Boynton Beach · Boca Raton · Palm Beach County**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Delivery** | Hand delivery, or buyer pickup. **Postage: $0.** |
| **Round trip** | Under ~1 hour for most of it |
| **Why first** | Best margin, best credibility, fastest trust, easiest to service |
| **Recommended minimum for free delivery** | **12 kits** — see §3 |
| **Also offer** | **Pickup in Delray Beach at any quantity, including 1.** Costs nothing and turns a $45.99 sale into a $45.99 sale with no postage. |

**Channels that fit Ring 1 best, in order:**

| Channel | Realistic order | Why it fits here specifically |
|---|---|---|
| **Camps & county / municipal parks-and-recreation programs** | 50–300 | Maya's home turf and home credential. Parks departments run camps with real per-camper supply budgets. |
| **Homeschool co-ops & micro-schools** | 15–60 | Fastest-deciding bulk buyer that exists. A co-op leader can say yes in one meeting. |
| **Coastal nature centers, sea-turtle and marine conservation centers, science centers** | 50–150 | Two budgets in one building: education programs *and* the gift shop. |
| **Private, charter and marine/environmental magnet school programs** | 24–100 | A single teacher can buy a class set; a private school head can buy a grade level. |
| **Dive shops, marinas, charter and water-sports businesses** | 6–24 | Lowest volume, **highest referral value.** These people know every camp and school program on the coast. |
| **Scout troops, 4-H, after-school clubs** | 10–30 | Fast, friendly, small. Good for filling gaps between larger orders. |
| **Libraries — county youth services** | 10–30 | Slow purchasing, small budget, but excellent local visibility. |

**How the offer differs in Ring 1:**
- Lead with **"I'll bring them to you."** That is the offer, not a footnote.
- Offer to **drop off one sample kit in person** rather than mail it — it costs almost nothing locally and puts Maya in the room.
- Offer a **short in-person session** with the kits as an upsell or a closing sweetener *(⚠️ this is Maya's time and must be capped and priced — Finance and Maya only)*.
- Price can be better than the shipped tiers **because the postage is not being spent.** Finance must set the number.

### 🟡 RING 2 — drivable with effort · **batch these**
**Broward / Fort Lauderdale · Miami-Dade · Treasure Coast (Martin, St. Lucie, Indian River)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Delivery** | Drivable, but a real half-day. Fuel, tolls, traffic, and a day Maya is not packing kits (risk R-05: everything depends on one person). |
| **Round trip** | ~1.5 – 3+ hours |
| **Recommended minimum for free delivery** | **24 kits** — and **only on a scheduled route day**, not a dedicated trip |
| **Recommended minimum for a dedicated same-week trip** | **50 kits** |

> ### 🟢 The operational recommendation that makes Ring 2 work: **route days, not trips**
> Do not drive to Broward because one order came in. Designate **one delivery day per fortnight** and tell Ring 2 buyers: *"I deliver to Broward and Miami-Dade every other Thursday — I can have these with you on the 9th or the 23rd."*
>
> This does three things at once: it batches the driving so two or three orders share one trip, it protects Maya's packing time, and **a stated delivery window reads as professional rather than improvised.** Institutional buyers prefer a date they can plan around to a vague "soon."

**Channels that fit Ring 2 best:** same list as Ring 1, but **skip the small ones.** Dive shops, scout troops and libraries are not worth a Broward drive on their own — they can be shipped, or added to a route day already happening.

**How the offer differs in Ring 2:**
- Delivery is free **at 24+**, on a route day.
- Below 24, it ships, at the standard confirmed prices.
- **Pooled orders count.** Two co-ops in the same town that combine to 24 get delivery. This is worth saying out loud in the script — it makes the buyer recruit the second buyer for Maya, at zero cost.

## 3. Recommended minimum order for free local delivery — with the reasoning

> ⚠️ **RECOMMENDATION ONLY. Not a price, not a policy, not an offer. Finance must check margin; Maya must approve before this is said to anybody.**

| Ring | Free delivery at | Below that |
|---|---|---|
| **Ring 1** — Palm Beach County | **12 kits** | Ships at confirmed prices — **or free pickup in Delray Beach at any quantity** |
| **Ring 2** — Broward / Miami-Dade / Treasure Coast | **24 kits, on a scheduled route day** (50 for a dedicated trip) | Ships at confirmed prices |
| **Ring 3** — everywhere else | Not offered | Ships; consolidate to one address where possible |

### Why 12 for Ring 1

1. **The postage saved pays for the drive many times over.** 12 × $8.85–$12.00 = **$106–$144 of postage avoided** on a trip under an hour. That is a good hourly return on Maya's time before the sale itself is counted.
2. **12 is already a real number in this business.** The 12-kit bundle at $503.99 is a CONFIRMED price point. Reusing it means no new tier to explain and no new number to defend.
3. **It is low enough to be reachable.** A single homeschool co-op class, a scout troop, or half a classroom clears 12. Set the bar at 24 and a lot of Ring 1's best small buyers fall below it.
4. **Below 12, pickup covers it.** Nobody is turned away — they are offered a different door.

### Why 24 + a route day for Ring 2

1. 24 × $8.85–$12.00 = **$212–$288 of postage avoided** — enough to justify a half-day, *if the day is shared between orders*.
2. A dedicated 3-hour round trip for one 24-kit order is a worse use of Maya's time than paying the postage. **The route day is what makes the number work.** Without batching, Ring 2's minimum should be 50.
3. Fulfilment depends entirely on one person (risk R-05). Every delivery day is a packing day lost. Batching is the mitigation.

### 🔴 What could make these numbers wrong
- **Postage is a stated range, not a measured figure.** The Click-N-Ship export (open question U-8, 10 minutes of Maya's time) replaces the whole range with a real number and firms up every line above.
- **COGS per kit is unknown.** If a kit costs more to make than anyone currently thinks, no delivery saving rescues a bad price.
- **Maya's time is not costed anywhere.** These thresholds treat her driving hours as free. They are not.

## 4. The honest limitation of the ring strategy

**Ring 1 will not supply 2,000 kits.**

Palm Beach County contains a finite number of camps, co-ops, nature centers and schools, and only a fraction of them will buy. Concentrating there buys **better margin and faster first wins** — it does not by itself buy volume.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ring 1 | 12 | 40 | 480 | 24% |
| Ring 2 | 8 | 40 | 320 | 16% |
| Ring 3 (incl. 1 corporate/sponsor) | 14 | 60 | 840 | 42% |
| Warm-list direct retail | — | 1 | ~40–80 | 2–4% |
| **Total** | | | **~1,700** | **~85%** |

> **The correct reading:** rings are a **sequencing and margin strategy**, not a sufficiency strategy. Work Ring 1 first because it is the most profitable and the most winnable. But 2,000 still requires Ring 3, and Ring 3 still requires postage — which is exactly why the **digital products and one-address consolidation matter so much for distant buyers.**
