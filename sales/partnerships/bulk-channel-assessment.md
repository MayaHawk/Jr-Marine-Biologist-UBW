**Owner:** Sales Manager · **Prepared:** 2026-09-19

**Metric it moves:** #3 **Kits committed in bulk orders** (and through it, #1 Kits sold).

> **Nothing in this file has been sent to anyone. No person, school, camp, museum or shop has been contacted. No relationship named here exists unless Maya says it does — no organisation is named anywhere in this file, deliberately.**

---

## 0. Plain-language glossary (used throughout)

| Term | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **Bulk buyer** | One person or organisation who buys many kits in one order — 24, 50, 100, 500 |
| **Decision maker** | The one person who can say yes and release money. Not always the person who answers the email. |
| **Budget cycle** | The time of year an organisation actually has money to spend and is choosing what to spend it on. Miss it and you wait a year. |
| **Procurement** | The formal purchasing process a school district or large institution uses. Forms, vendor registration, purchase orders. Slow. |
| **COI (certificate of insurance)** | A one-page proof of product liability insurance. Many institutions ask for it before they can buy anything. |
| **Wholesale** | Selling to a shop that resells. The shop needs a margin, so you get roughly half the retail price. |
| **Lead time** | How long between the order and the delivery. Bulk buyers usually accept a long one if you tell them up front. |
| **Pipeline** | The list of live conversations, each at a known stage. Not a list of hopes. |

### 1b. 🔴 No product liability insurance is a *sales* blocker, not just a risk

**ASSUMPTION A-021:** schools, districts, libraries, museums, many camps and most retail wholesale accounts commonly require a vendor to provide a **certificate of insurance** before they can purchase. *(General commercial practice, labelled as an assumption because it has not been verified for any specific buyer here.)*

If that assumption holds, then **several of the channels below are closed until insurance exists** — no matter how good the outreach is. Risk R-01 already recommends getting quotes (researched range ~$300–$800/yr for $1M cover, a researched range and **not a quote**). That action has now moved from "prudent" to "**may be the gate on the largest orders in the plan.**"

**This is the cheapest thing on this page and possibly the highest-leverage. One broker call.**

## 3. Why wholesale is ranked last, in money

**ESTIMATE.** *Inputs: 2,000 kits (goal, CONFIRMED). $39.58/kit = the confirmed 24-kit bundle rate. $23.00/kit = 50% of the confirmed $45.99 retail, using the conventional wholesale split (ASSUMPTION A-026).*

| Route | Per kit | 2,000 kits |
|---|---|---|
| 24-bundle rate | $39.58 | **$79,160** |
| Wholesale at half retail | $23.00 | **$46,000** |
| **Difference** | | **−$33,160** |

Wholesale gives away **roughly a third of the total** before a single cost is counted — and **COGS per kit is still unknown**, so nobody can currently say whether $23.00 is profit or loss. Until Finance has a real per-kit cost, quoting a wholesale price is guessing with the company's money.

**Recommendation:** keep wholesale closed until `finance/cogs/sharks.md` has real numbers in it. It is not a fast channel anyway.
