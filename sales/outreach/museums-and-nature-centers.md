## SCRIPT 1 — Ring 1 education director, first contact

**Subject line options:**
- `Marine science kits for your camp programmes — I'm local`
- `From a marine educator in Delray Beach`
- `A take-home for your [sea turtle / reef / shark] programme`

Hi [name],

I'm a marine science and environmental education graduate from Eckerd College, a scuba instructor and a boat captain, and I ran ocean adventure camps here in South Florida. I'm based in Delray Beach.

I make Jr. Marine Biologist kits — one ocean topic per box. Sharks, sea turtles, coral reefs, mangroves, ocean zones, cephalopods. Each kit has five hands-on activities: one science, one technology, one engineering, one art and one math. Plus collectible flash cards and a named certificate.

I'm writing to you rather than to your shop, because the use I think is most interesting is on the programme side — a take-home for your camp weeks, or something that goes out with an outreach visit so the science doesn't stop when the child leaves the building.

Two things you'll care about that a catalogue won't tell you:

The science is written by someone who has actually taught it in the field, and I'll happily send you the content for a topic so you can check it yourself before you believe me.

And I'm ten minutes up the road — **I deliver these myself, within a week.** No lead times, no freight, no materials that arrive after your programme starts.

Could I bring one in for you to look at?

Maya Hawk
Jr. Marine Biologist

## SCRIPT 2 — The topic-matched version 🟢 *(the strongest variant)*

*Replace the third paragraph of Script 1 with a line naming what the institution actually does:*

> I noticed [the sea turtle programme / the reef exhibit / your shark week] — I make a kit on exactly that. It would go home with a child who just spent a morning with you and keep the subject alive for another week.

> **Why this is the strongest version.** It stops being "a kit" and becomes *their subject, in a box, by a marine scientist.* It also proves Maya looked, which most vendor email does not.
>
> ⚠️ **Only write this line from something publicly and currently stated by the institution** — their own website or published programme listing. **Never from memory and never from an assumption.** Getting an institution's own programme wrong destroys the credibility the rest of the email is built on.
