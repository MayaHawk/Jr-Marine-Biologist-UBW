## SCRIPT 1 — Ring 1 camp, first contact

**Send to:** the camp director or owner by name
**Subject line options:**
- `From one camp director to another`
- `A marine science kit for your rainy days — I'm in Delray Beach`
- `Ocean kits for [camp's] campers — local, delivered in a week`

---

Hi [name],

I ran ocean adventure camps here in South Florida. Marine science degree from Eckerd, scuba instructor, boat captain — most of my working life has been spent getting kids' hands into the water.

The problem I could never solve was the rainy afternoon. Or the bus day. Or the week the water activity got cancelled and I needed something real for forty kids in twenty minutes.

So I built the thing I wanted. Jr. Marine Biologist kits — one ocean topic per box. Sharks. Sea turtles. Coral reefs. Each kit has five hands-on activities in it: one science, one technology, one engineering, one art and one math. Plus collectible flash cards and a certificate with the camper's name on it.

It is real marine science. It is not a craft project with a fish on it.

I'm writing because I'm in Delray Beach, and I think [camp] might use these the way I would have — one per camper as the take-home, or a topic per session.

Two things that matter if you're local: **I deliver these myself, within a week.** No shipping, no tracking number, no wondering whether your materials turn up before the session starts.

If it's useful, I'd rather bring you one to open than describe it in an email. Would that be worth twenty minutes?

Maya Hawk
Jr. Marine Biologist
