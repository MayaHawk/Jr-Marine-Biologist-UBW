# research/ — Market & Competitor Research

**Owner:** `market-researcher`

## What belongs here
Audience research · competitor profiles · demand assessments · the customer language bank · opportunity briefs.

## Structure
```
research/
├── audience/        ← who buys, why, objections, and their actual words
├── competitors/     ← one file per competitor + comparison.md
├── demand/          ← "would this kit actually sell?" assessments
└── opportunities/   ← underserved gaps worth pursuing
```

## The rule for this folder
Every fact needs a **source and a date searched**. Anything without one is an assumption and must be labeled. **No invented statistics, reviews, competitors, or prices — ever.**
