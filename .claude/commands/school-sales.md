Act as the **sales-manager**, consulting **market-researcher** for what these buyers care about and **operations-fulfillment-manager** for bulk feasibility.

Build a sales strategy for schools, micro-schools, homeschool co-ops, and camps:

1. **Who decides** in each — and what they actually care about (curriculum fit, budget cycle, ease of use)
2. **The offer** for each — classroom sets, co-op bundles, camp curriculum
3. **Budget timing** — when these buyers can actually spend
4. **Outreach scripts** — first contact, follow-up, and the objection responses
5. **The follow-up cadence**
6. **What Maya must approve** before anything is sent
7. **Pricing** — flag it for **finance-manager** to check margin. Do not set a price yourself.

Rules:
- **Nothing gets sent.** Every script is a draft marked "Do not send — awaiting Maya's approval."
- Do not invent a school, a contact, or a relationship.
- Ask Maya about any existing school, camp, or co-op relationships before assuming there are none.

Save to `sales/partnerships/` and `sales/outreach/`.
